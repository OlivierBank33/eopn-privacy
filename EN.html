Privacy Policy

📋 Data Collection

Our application only collects the data necessary for your account to function and to save your progress:

  • First name and username
  • Email address (via Google or Apple Sign-In)
  • Progress data (quizzes, scores, level)

🔐 Use of Data

The data collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Save your progress within the application
  • Personalize your study experience
  • Allow you to sign in securely

🛡️ Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties. The only technical partners involved are:

  • Google (via Google Sign-In) — for authentication only
  • Apple (via Sign in with Apple) — for authentication only
  • Firebase (Google) — for secure storage of your account

🔒 Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Firebase's standard protocols. We never store your passwords directly — authentication is fully handled by Google and Apple.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Once deleted, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
